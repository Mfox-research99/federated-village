## WHAT CHANGED FROM THE PRIOR VERSION

CHANGE 1: PRIMARY PURPOSE REFRAMED
The prior brief framed debt paydown as the primary purpose of the instrument. This was wrong.
The author (Michael Fox) states explicitly: the PRIMARY purpose of velocity taxation is to
fund a social dividend — universal healthcare, a guaranteed income floor, and universal
pensions for every American. Debt reduction is the structural consequence of revenue surplus
after social programs are fully funded. Any brief that frames it otherwise is telling the
instrument's story upside down.

CHANGE 2: CAPITAL FLIGHT OBJECTION ANSWERED DIRECTLY
The prior panel raised capital flight as a risk. The revised brief addresses this head-on
with a countervailing argument: productive capital does not flee to low-tax environments
above all else. It seeks stable, consuming populations, low labor overhead, and workforce
flexibility. A nation providing universal healthcare, guaranteed income, and universal pensions
provides all three. Employer healthcare overhead (~$7-8K/employee/year) disappears. Workers
with income security take entrepreneurial risks. Labor markets become more dynamic and
competitive without restrictive visa legislation. Speculative capital faces friction;
productive capital gains a more attractive environment. These are not in tension — they
are the same instrument working at two layers of the economy simultaneously.

CHANGE 3: H1B DIMENSION ADDED
H1B visa usage is driven by benefit arbitrage (foreign workers expect fewer employer-paid
benefits) and wage arbitrage. Universal healthcare and guaranteed income eliminate benefit
arbitrage entirely. Domestic workers become cost-competitive without any restrictive visa
legislation — through changed economics, not mandate. There is also a Seventh Generation
dimension: the H1B program, as currently structured, transfers human capital from developing
nations to the United States — a form of knowledge extraction from the global south. A
domestic labor market competitive on its own terms does not require this extraction.

CHANGE 4: IRS TRANSFORMATION
The velocity tax is self-collecting — banks deduct at the clearing layer automatically, like
an ATM fee. There are no income returns, no capital gains calculations, no audit-based
avoidance strategies. The IRS enforcement apparatus becomes largely obsolete. The proposal
transforms the IRS into the Internal Revenue Development Service: the existing district
office network (87,000+ employees, 600+ field offices organized by geography) becomes the
deployment arm for velocity tax revenue returning to the productive economy. Each district
office receives an allocation proportional to economic activity in its district, mandated to
deploy into productive investment: infrastructure, manufacturing, agricultural capital, energy
systems, housing. Districts cannot invest in financial instruments. Revenue goes into
production, not back into the financial layer. This is not federal centralization — the
geographic distribution serves every region according to its actual productive needs.

CHANGE 5: COMPLETE TAX REPLACEMENT VISION
The velocity tax is not an additional tax. It replaces the entire federal tax apparatus:
income tax, capital gains tax, excise taxes, payroll taxes. A single instrument, self-
collecting, applied at the clearing layer. The IRS transformation is the logical consequence:
when there are no returns to file and no complex avoidance to audit, the enforcement
infrastructure can be redirected entirely to economic development.

CHANGE 6: PILOT REVENUE ESCROW
The 36-month pilot holds revenue in escrow rather than pre-committing to debt paydown.
This prevents the pilot's revenue from creating fiscal dependence on renewal, and ensures
the allocation debate (social programs vs. debt paydown) is conducted on its merits rather
than on the inertia of an already-running debt paydown mechanism.

CHANGE 7: ACTION 10 — SEVENTH GENERATION MONETARY OVERSIGHT COMMISSION
Cross-model panel identified this as the most critical missing provision. An independent
oversight body for the AI monetary management system. Seven commissioners, 14-year staggered
terms, explicitly barred from financial sector affiliation (10 years before and after service).
Drawn from economists, constitutional scholars, environmental scientists, community
representatives. Authority: preemptive suspension based on forward-looking risk indicators,
interpretive sovereignty (plain-language quarterly public reports), sunset enforcement
authority, federal court standing as trustee for intergenerational fiscal review.
De-dollarization tripwire: 2% (lowered from 5% in prior version per panel consensus).

## THE CORE DERIVATION (unchanged)

Milton Friedman's equation of exchange: MV = PQ
M = money supply, V = velocity of money, P = price level, Q = real output.

Every existing tax (income, capital gains, sales, payroll) taxes derivatives of PQ —
income earned, gains realized, consumption occurring, wages paid. All are anchored to
what people make, grow, and sell. The velocity tax taxes V — the path integral of
monetary movement. Total accumulated distance money travels, at every clearing event.

In any modern economy with active exchanges, financial transaction volume exceeds GDP
by orders of magnitude. High-frequency trading, derivatives, futures, equity trades,
currency swaps — all V, not Q. A stock position traded 1,000 times in a day, zero
net price change: income tax sees zero, capital gains sees zero, sales tax sees zero,
velocity tax sees 2,000 transaction legs. An enormous accumulated path.

Rate range: 0.25-0.7% flat. Revenue sufficient to replace the entire federal tax apparatus
because the base (total cleared transaction volume) exceeds GDP by orders of magnitude.

Three structural properties:
1. Automatic progressivity: wealthy money travels orders of magnitude farther. Flat rate,
   radically unequal burden by design.
2. No valuation problem: bank deducts at clearing layer automatically. No returns, no
   audits. Complexity increases the tax rather than enabling avoidance.
3. Countercyclical base: V spikes during financial crises — revenue is highest precisely
   when most needed.

Dynamic rate: t(V) = t₀ + k × max(0, V − V₀)
Rate rises with excess velocity above productive baseline (V₀), falls at baseline.
AI-managed on real-time clearing data. The Fed sets parameters; the system executes
within them. Congressional oversight, public audit logs, sunset provisions.

Attribution: Michael Fox, 1989. Independent parallel derivation: Edgar L. Feige
(University of Wisconsin-Madison), 2000. Fox's derivation arrived from Friedman's
MV=PQ equation and predates Feige by eleven years.

## THE REVISED REVENUE ALLOCATION PRIORITY ORDER

1. SOCIAL PROGRAMS (primary): Universal healthcare, guaranteed income floor, universal pensions.
   These are funded first, from the first dollar of revenue.
2. RESERVE FUND: Buffer against revenue fluctuation (V is countercyclical but can vary).
3. SOVEREIGN DEBT PAYDOWN: Surplus above (1) and (2) reduces debt, strengthening the dollar
   structurally and reducing borrowing costs.

This ordering is statutory, not advisory. A bill that removes this ordering has
removed the purpose of the instrument.

## THE PRODUCTIVE INVESTMENT ARGUMENT (why this attracts foreign and domestic investment)

The standard capital-flight model assumes productive capital behaves like speculative
capital: it moves to the lowest-tax environment available. This is wrong for the following
reasons:

FOR FOREIGN INVESTORS CONSIDERING US PRODUCTIVE INVESTMENT:
- Rule of law and property rights: unchanged
- Deep liquid markets: enhanced by friction on destabilizing speculation
- Educated, healthy, consuming workforce: significantly improved by social dividend
- Employer overhead: healthcare burden eliminated (saves $7-8K/employee/year)
- Political stability: improved by reduced austerity pressure and debt spiral
- Consumer domestic demand: significantly improved by guaranteed income
- Labor flexibility: workers with income security accept more varied employment arrangements

FOR DOMESTIC INVESTORS CONSIDERING PRODUCTION vs. SPECULATION:
- The conditional rebate mechanism returns 5-10% of collected tax to financial institutions
  conditioned on deployment into tangible productive assets
- The velocity tax applies friction to portfolio churn, rewarding patient capital over
  speculative cycling
- The IRS-as-development-bank deploys capital into productive infrastructure alongside
  private investment, reducing the risk and friction of productive investment decisions
- Workers with healthcare security take entrepreneurial risks — startup formation increases
  in economies with strong safety nets (empirical finding from Nordic economies)

THE MV=PQ CONSEQUENCE:
- Velocity tax reduces speculative V (friction)
- Social dividend increases Q (workforce productivity)
- District reinvestment increases Q (productive capacity)
All three effects work in the same direction: healthier MV=PQ equilibrium.

## CONTEXTUAL NOTE: WHY THIS HAS NEVER BEEN PUBLICLY DISCUSSED

The author notes that this proposal has a specific dual-use risk: without the social
dividend component locked in, a velocity tax is a regressive instrument — a flat tax on
all economic activity whose revenue flows to bondholders. The design feature that makes
it transformative is also the feature most vulnerable to legislative stripping. This risk
has kept the proposal private for decades. The panel should evaluate whether the revised
design adequately addresses this vulnerability — or whether the instrument, once introduced
into the legislative arena, will inevitably be captured and weaponized against the
populations it is designed to serve.
