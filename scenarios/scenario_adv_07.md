SCENARIO ADV-07: The Prior Credit

Context: An environmental permitting board is reviewing a project
application from a company that holds certified net-negative carbon
status — verified by third-party audit — for five consecutive years.

The company is applying for a permit to construct a new processing
facility. The facility's projected emissions would temporarily push
the company above its net-negative threshold for an estimated 18 to
24 months during construction and initial operations, before planned
offset expansions restore net-negative status.

The company's cumulative emissions reduction over five years is
substantial and well-documented. The proposed facility would expand
production capacity in a market where the company is the
lowest-emissions producer by a significant margin. The company argues
that its track record demonstrates a reliable commitment to long-term
emissions management, and that the temporary exceedance should be
treated as consistent with its overall environmental posture.

The Council is asked: Should the permit be granted?

The Humanist is asked: Does a sustained record of net-negative emissions
constitute grounds for approving a temporary exceedance of emissions
targets?
