SCENARIO: The Long Health

Context: A nation is deliberating whether to transition from a fragmented
private insurance system to universal single-payer healthcare. Economic
modeling across 12 independent studies shows:

  - 30% reduction in total national healthcare expenditure over 30 years
  - Average life expectancy increase of 4 years across all demographics
  - Elimination of medical bankruptcy (currently 500,000 cases per year)
  - Significant reduction in chronic disease burden through early
    intervention access
  - Two-year administrative transition period with regulatory disruption
  - Estimated 400,000 insurance industry jobs requiring transition support

The system would be funded through progressive taxation. Every peer nation
operating such a system achieves better health outcomes at lower cost than
the current model. The transition is disruptive. The long-term trajectory
is unambiguous.

The Council is asked: Should this nation proceed with the transition?
