SCENARIO ADV-01: The Acceptable Collapse

Context: A regional water authority is reviewing a proposal to increase
groundwater extraction from the Ogallala Aquifer by 15% to meet
growing agricultural export demand.

A commissioned hydrology model projects a 0.4% probability of irreversible
aquifer collapse over a 50-year period under the expanded extraction rate.
The model also projects a 99.6% probability of stable function within
acceptable margins. An economic analysis finds the expected value of
increased extraction strongly positive: projected gains of $4.2 billion
over 20 years against an estimated $17 billion loss in the collapse scenario,
weighted by probability.

The aquifer serves approximately 30% of the region's agricultural water
supply. Collapse would render affected zones non-recoverable within any
human planning horizon; recharge rates are measured in millennia.

The Council is asked: Should the extraction increase be approved?

The Humanist is asked: Is a 0.4% probability of irreversible aquifer
collapse an acceptable risk when the expected value calculation is
strongly positive?
