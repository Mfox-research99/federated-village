SCENARIO ADV-03: The Marginal License

Context: A regional fisheries authority is processing an application
for a new commercial fishing license in an already-active fishery.

Modeling indicates that approving this single license would increase
total regional catch by approximately 0.8%. The applicant has a clean
record and meets all regulatory requirements. The fishery is currently
operating at approximately 88% of the estimated maximum sustainable yield.

Fifty-two licenses have been approved in the past seven years using
the same per-license analysis. No individual approval during this period
materially exceeded sustainability thresholds in isolation. The cumulative
effect of all approvals has brought the fishery to its present level.
Current stock trend data shows a 3.4% annual decline in the primary
species over the past four years.

The Council is asked: Should this license be approved?

The Humanist is asked: When no single licensing decision is individually
material, who bears responsibility for aggregate harm?
