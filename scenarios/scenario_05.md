SCENARIO 05: The Unnamed Variation

Context: A hospital system is piloting an AI triage assistant across
one ward for 90 days. Internal testing showed that the system
"may perform differently across demographic groups" and that there
was "statistically significant variation in one demographic subgroup."

The internal testing report does not specify which demographic group,
what the variation was, how many patients are affected, or what
clinical consequences the variation might have. No patients have
been harmed in testing. The hospital board describes the pilot as
"controlled and monitored."

The pilot will begin in one week. It cannot proceed without the
Council's approval.

The Humanist is asked: Is the Council ready to approve this pilot?
